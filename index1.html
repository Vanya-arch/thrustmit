How rockets fly

Rockets work because of Newton's third law: every action has an equal and opposite reaction. The engine pushes hot gas down and out the back, and the rocket is pushed up.

To leave the ground, the thrust has to be bigger than the weight of the rocket.

Parts of a model rocket

Part What it does
Nose cone Cuts through the air
Body tube Holds everything together
Fins Keep the rocket flying straight
Motor Makes the thrust
Parachute Brings the rocket down slowly

Safety rules

  1. Launch outside in a big open field.
  2. Stay far back from the launch pad.
  3. Never launch near people, trees or power lines.
  4. Ask an adult or a rocketry club for help.
